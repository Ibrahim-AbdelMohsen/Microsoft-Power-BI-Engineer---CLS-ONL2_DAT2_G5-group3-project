# Graduation Project: Store Sales Dataset Analysis

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a store sales dataset using Power BI . The aim is to derive insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, and regional analysis to support decision-making processes.

## Project Steps

1. **Data Import**: Imported the superstore sales CSV file into Power BI.
2. **Data Transformation**: Used Power Query to clean and preprocess data.
   - Change data types, trim text, and filter rows.
   - Created dimensions for customer and product.
   - Added unique identifiers for locations.
3. **Data Modeling**: Built a data model to join all relevant tables.
4. **Visualizations**: Created various dashboards to visualize insights on:
   - Sales performance
   - Customer segmentation
   - Regional analysis

## Key Insights

- **Sales Performance**:
  - Total sales reached 2.25M with a growth rate of 20.76%.
  - Technology products lead in sales, while items like Fasteners lag behind.
  
- **Customer Insights**:
  - Top customers significantly contribute to overall revenue.
  - Consumer and Corporate segments dominate sales.

- **Regional Analysis**:
  - The Central region has the highest total sales.
  - California, New York, and Texas are identified as major sales hotspots.

## Business Questions Addressed

1. What are the total sales, product numbers, and growth rate?
2. Which product categories have the highest and lowest sales?
3. How does sales performance vary by shipping mode?
4. Who are the top customers contributing to overall sales?
5. Which regions are the most profitable?
